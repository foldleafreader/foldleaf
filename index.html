PDF and EPUB, with pages that turn.

A reader for the iPad and iPhone that treats a document as paper: pages that turn with a curl, margins you can crop away once for a whole document, and a pen and highlighter that work the way a pencil does on a page.

What it does

  • Pages that turn. A page curl for PDFs, single or facing, or plain scrolling if you prefer.
  • Crop the margins once. A paper's wasted white space gone for the whole document, not page by page.
  • PDF and EPUB in one library, with the same bookmarks, highlights and reading positions.
  • Apple Pencil throughout. Double-tap swaps the pen for its rubber, or the highlighter for its own.
  • Controls down the side, optionally — a page is taller than it is wide.
  • Paper colours, including a warm newsprint for reading at night.
