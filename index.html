A reader that treats a document as paper. Pages turn with a curl. Margins crop away once, for good. And the pen stays where you put it.

Crop View

Most papers waste a third of every page.

Draw a rectangle round what matters, once, and the whole PDF opens that way from then on. Zoom Lock keeps your framing steady as the pages turn.

Apple Pencil

A pen that behaves like one.

Pen, fountain pen, pencil and marker, each answering pressure and tilt in its own way. Hold still at the end of a line and it snaps straight. Double-tap the Pencil for the rubber.

Built in

Papers and books, a tap away.

Browse arXiv and save a paper named for its authors and title. Save any of Project Gutenberg’s tens of thousands of free books as an EPUB. Or bring your own from Files.

Library

Everything in its place.

Folders, favourites and recents, shown by their covers. Search titles, authors and the books themselves. Deleted books wait a week in the Bin, in case you change your mind.

Made for reading

Read it your way.

Light or dark, controls along the top or down the side, and every page a tap away.
