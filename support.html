Reporting something that went wrong

Four things turn a report into a fix, and without them it is mostly guesswork:

  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • The app's version and build — in the app, tap the gear, then Info.
  • Your device and iOS version — Settings → General → About.
  • The document, if you can share it and it is not confidential. Most awkward bugs live inside one particular file.

Common questions

Does it sync between devices?

Not at present. Your library is included in your device's own backup, so it comes back with the rest of your iPad's data if you restore it.

Where are my books kept?

Importing copies the file into the app's own storage, so deleting the original elsewhere does not affect your library. The app's folder is visible in the Files app.

Will my highlights survive an update?

Yes. Annotations, bookmarks and reading positions are stored alongside each book and carried across updates.

Is there a Mac version?

Not at present.

Requests

Feature requests are welcome and are read, though not all of them can be built. Say what you are trying to do rather than how you think it should work — the problem is more useful than the proposed solution.
